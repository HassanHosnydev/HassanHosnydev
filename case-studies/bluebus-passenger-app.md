# BlueBus passenger app

A React Native bus-booking application for Android and iOS, published on Google Play and the App Store.

## My role

I developed and maintained the BlueBus passenger application and worked on its Android and iOS releases. This is the customer booking app, separate from the internal driver applications distributed directly as APK files.

## My contribution

My work includes the Arabic/English experience and ticket-management flows. The reviewed history records changes for ticket history, ticket cancellation, ticket editing, and localization. A selected localization diff shows translation integration across destination, booking, and ticket screens.

The reviewed package manifest includes React Native with Expo, React Navigation, Apollo Client and GraphQL tooling, Axios, NativeWind, i18next, and react-i18next. The historical branch reviewed here is not presented as a complete account of every later release.
